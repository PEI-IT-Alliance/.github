# Contributing

Thanks for helping out. Here's how changes land in PEI IT Alliance repos.

1. Open an issue first for anything bigger than a typo, so we agree on the approach.
2. Fork the repo and create a branch from `main`.
3. Keep pull requests small and focused on one change.
4. Fill in the pull request template.
5. A member of Alliance staff reviews and merges.

New here? Come say hi at an event: https://www.peiitalliance.com/events

Everyone who contributes follows our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
